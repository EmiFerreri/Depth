# DEPTH — Story Bible

## Core truth

DEPTH is not merely a prison, simulation or testing facility.

It is a machine created to preserve something that cannot be stored as ordinary information: the way a consciousness makes choices.

The builders discovered that memory alone was insufficient. Identity lived in how a mind reacted to uncertainty, risk, freedom, loss, movement and choice.

The sphere is not a copied person. It is an incomplete seed of consciousness.

DEPTH is not trying to discover who the sphere was.

It is trying to discover whether it can become someone.
